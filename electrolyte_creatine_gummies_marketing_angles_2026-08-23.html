Avatar

18-60, unisex, active health-optimizer in the Biohacking and Longevity niche. Already takes (or should take) both creatine and electrolytes but runs them as two separate powders: a shaker bottle for creatine, a mix packet or drink for electrolytes. Price-aware, taste-sensitive, and increasingly skeptical of gummy supplements after hearing that many creatine gummy brands under-dose the label claim.

Before

Two separate supplements, a daily mixing ritual, inconsistent taste, and quiet doubt about whether the "easy" gummy format is really dosed right.

After

One gummy that verifiably delivers both real creatine and real electrolytes, no shaker, no chalky taste, no guessing.

Biggest objection

Is this gummy actually dosed with real creatine and electrolytes, or is it a candy-coated placebo like the brands I've read got called out for under-dosing?

Pain points

  • Taste divisiveness on existing electrolyte gummies: verified 3-star review, "They feel like they work to hydrate but they do not taste very good." Amazon, Plant People WonderHydrate (B0FCHG5DH2)
  • Price sensitivity even from satisfied buyers: verified review, "I love the taste... The cost is a little pricey." Amazon, B0FCHG5DH2
  • Dosage-fraud skepticism specific to creatine gummies: "99% of creatine gummies have no creatine in them." Slickdeals user comment, creatine gummies deal thread, Aug 2026
  • Water retention / bloat fear as a reason people avoid creatine, especially outside the bodybuilder identity. ubiehealth.com; bouncenutrition.com
  • Digestive discomfort (cramping, bloating) reported as dose-dependent. consistentreviews.com/creatine-gummies-side-effects
  • Cost-per-serving objection vs. bulk powder ($0.98+ vs $0.61-$1.25/serving). bouncenutrition.com/blogs/news/electrolyte-gummies-vs-powder

Benefits

  • Quick-acting hydration during activity: verified 5-star review, "when I feel low on electrolytes I reach for these and they work pretty quickly." Amazon, B0FCHG5DH2
  • Taste as a category-switch driver: verified 5-star review, "This is the first electrolyte that I actually want to take." Amazon, B0FCHG5DH2
  • No mixing, travel-convenient format vs. powders and shaker bottles. Amazon listing bullets, B0FCHG5DH2
  • Adherence and satisfaction data favors gummy format: 91.7% adherence and 9.1/10 satisfaction vs. 78.4% and 6.3/10 for monohydrate powder. beStrong Creatine Gummies Study (n=140)
  • Broadening age relevance: lean muscle, energy, and cognitive-recovery framing resonates beyond the traditional bodybuilder demographic. accio.com
