Research Notes

Top Pain Points

  • Gritty, non-dissolving powder that leaves chalky residue or film
  • Fishy smell/taste and “fish burps” from marine-sourced collagen
  • Forgetting to take it daily / compliance friction ("clumpy mess")
  • No visible results after months of consistent use → placebo skepticism
  • Digestive side effects reported on some products (bloating, cramping)
  • Price vs. uncertain payoff (“am I paying for expensive flavored water?”)
  • Distrust of company-funded studies and hype-heavy marketing claims
Grounded in Amazon review aggregators (Garage Gym Reviews, Medino, Honest Brand Reviews), WebMD user reviews, Trustpilot, Consumer Reports, and curated Reddit consensus (direct Reddit/YouTube fetches were JS-blocked; pivoted to aggregator and review-site sources per stop-conditions).

Top Benefits (when it works)

  • Skin feels smoother, more radiant, fine lines less noticeable (weeks 4–8)
  • Hair growing faster/stronger; nails less brittle
  • Joint comfort improvements (secondary benefit, not our primary claim)
  • Fizzy tablet fully dissolves vs. gritty powder (direct format solve)
  • No fishy smell/taste since our formula is vegan
  • Simple daily ritual (drop tablet, drink, done) solves forgetting
Real usage-timeline quotes from WebMD reviews and Amazon-review aggregators; benefit language kept to what buyers actually report, not clinical claims we cannot substantiate.
