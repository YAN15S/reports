What the research said

Top pain points

  • "No effect on me at all" / "I suspect it's not going to work", the dominant skepticism theme across Amazon 1-3 star and Reddit.
  • Price objection: "$75-125/month" seen as expensive relative to an unproven personal result; several say "worth trying once, won't rebuy at this price."
  • Slow, unclear results timeline: clinical trials measure over 4 months, but buyers expect to feel something in 2-8 weeks and often don't.
  • Confusion vs. other longevity supplements already tried and abandoned (NMN, NAD+, CoQ10); supplement fatigue is real.
  • Minor logistics/side-effect complaints: softgel leakage in shipping, mild GI discomfort, occasional afternoon energy dips, near-expiry stock.
  • Only an estimated 30-40% of people's gut microbiome efficiently converts food-based ellagitannins into Urolithin A, which most buyers don't know.

Top benefits

  • Muscle strength and endurance gains (12-15% in trials over weeks to months).
  • Clinically documented cellular/mitochondrial renewal via mitophagy (Nature Aging trials).
  • Faster recovery, reduced post-workout soreness, anti-inflammatory effect (lower CRP).
  • Real reported energy and HRV improvements from actual users, not just marketing copy.
  • A distinct illness-recovery / chronic-fatigue subgroup reporting shortened PEM (post-exertional malaise) episodes and more "good days," an unmarketed benefit pocket.
