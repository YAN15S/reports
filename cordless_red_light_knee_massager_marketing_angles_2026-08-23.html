Research Notes

Top Pain Points

  • Chronic ache flares on standing and stairs; morning stiffness is the worst part of the day.
  • Sleep disruption, can't get comfortable, wakes up sore (about a third of arthritis sufferers report this).
  • Already tried acetaminophen (minimal relief per Harvard Health) and glucosamine (no measurable benefit per a published Arthritis & Rheumatology study).
  • Fear of surgery: arthroscopic surgery does not improve degenerative knee arthritis and may raise future knee-replacement risk (BMJ).
  • Existing cheap knee wraps run cooler than advertised, straps too short for larger legs, and batteries die fast (consumer review aggregator).
  • Identity cost: activity and mood both suffer, the quiet feeling of "getting old before her time."

Top Benefits

  • Reported rapid relief within minutes of use, and reduced morning stiffness.
  • Better range of motion and flexibility from the heat component.
  • Reported use in post-knee-replacement recovery routines.
  • Thin, discreet, cordless design, wearable under clothing during the day.
  • Triple therapy (heat, 660nm red light, vibration/compression) in one device, three independently-supported modalities most single-mode competitors do not combine.
  • FSA/HSA eligible, feels like already-earmarked money rather than a new expense.
