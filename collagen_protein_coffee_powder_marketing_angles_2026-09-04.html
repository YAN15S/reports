Before

After

Biggest objection

"I've tried collagen/wellness products before and they did nothing but drain my wallet and get abandoned in a cabinet, why would this be different?"

Pain points

  • Forgetting to take a separate collagen powder/gummy; abandoned tubs in the cabinet (Amazon, forums)
  • Taste inconsistency: some find the chocolate mocha too sweet (Stevia), others find it bland; doesn't dissolve well in cold drinks (Amazon reviews)
  • Skepticism it "does nothing" / "just marketing hype," results take 8-12 weeks not overnight (Reddit-adjacent forums, YouTube Q&A)
  • "Does heat from coffee destroy the collagen?", the single most repeated objection (YouTube, blogs)
  • One reviewer flagged no published third-party heavy-metal testing on the chocolate variant, a real quality-transparency concern to route to Compliance/QC, not used in ad copy this run

Benefits

  • 5 collagen types (I, II, III, V, X) for skin, hair, nails, joints in one scoop (product listing)
  • Real usage signal: faster nail growth, reduced joint stiffness, smoother skin reported after 4-8 weeks of consistent use (Amazon, forums)
  • Removes the separate-purchase/separate-step friction by riding an existing coffee habit (Product DB Index finding, corroborated by forum "I just add it to my coffee and don't think about it" quotes)
  • Backed by real RCTs: 5-10g hydrolyzed collagen daily improves skin hydration/elasticity/wrinkle depth in 4-12 weeks; peptides are stable well past coffee temperature (572F degradation point vs. ~185F coffee)
