Research Notes

Top Pain Points

  • Pain follows sufferers into every position: sitting, standing, lying down, with no safe position left
  • "Tried everything" fatigue: PT, chiropractors, steroid injections, heating pads, all temporary or ineffective
  • Feeling trapped between "take more pills" and "eventually you'll need surgery"
  • Doctor visits that end in "here's more pain meds" with no real progress on the underlying cause
  • Single-therapy devices "pull right back": chiro decompresses but muscles re-tighten within hours; heat boosts blood flow but doesn't create disc space; massage relaxes muscle but the disc stays compressed
  • Deep skepticism that an at-home gadget can touch real nerve/disc compression rather than just surface muscle tightness
  • Practical gadget frustrations from comparable products: weak heat output, short battery life, overly firm massage nodes

Top Benefits

  • Traction physically creates space for a compressed nerve, distinct from muscle-only massage
  • Combining traction, heat and vibration closes the "does one thing" gap that makes single therapies wear off fast
  • Real buyer language on comparable tri-therapy devices: relief described as "a pressure valve opening up," ability to postpone or avoid surgery consultations, sitting through a movie again without shifting
  • Fits a 15-minute evening routine, low friction, can multitask (light household tasks) during use
  • At-home and private: no clinic visit, no explaining the pain to a stranger
